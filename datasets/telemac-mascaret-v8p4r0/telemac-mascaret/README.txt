The TELEMAC-MASCARET system is an integrated suite of solvers for use in the
field of free-surface flow. Having been used in the context of many studies
throughout the world and the subject of many peer-reviewed publications, it
has become one of the major standards in its field.
The TELEMAC-MASCARET system is managed by a consortium of organisations:
Artelia (France), BundesAnstalt für Wasserbau (BAW, Germany), Centre d'études
et d'expertise sur les risques, l'environnement, la mobilité et l'aménagement
(CEREMA, France), Electricité de France R&D (EDF, France), and HR Wallingford
(United Kingdom). It is further strengthened scientifically by Daresbury
Laboratory (United Kingdom), and the Centre Européen de Recherche et de 
Formation Avancée en Calcul Scientifique (CERFACS, France)

The TELEMAC-MASCARET system is used by most engineers for dimensioning and
impact studies, where safety is prevailing and, for this reason, reliability,
validation and a worldwide recognition of our tools are of utmost importance.
As a consequence and to improve the access to TELEMAC-MASCARET for the whole
community of consultants and researchers, the choice of open source has been
made. Anyone can thus take advantage of the TELEMAC-MASCARET system and assess
its performances and will find necessary resources on this website. Besides,
the quality of assistance, maintenance and hotline support are also very
important to professional users, and a special effort has been made to offer
alternatively a broad range of fee-paying services.
